Auto Dimension Pro

Revit Add-in for Autodesk® Revit®

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

1. Overview

Auto Dimension Pro is a desktop add-in for Autodesk® Revit®. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the plugin and how it is handled.

The short version: no data is ever sent to any server. Everything the plugin does stays on your machine.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Auto Dimension Pro does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, usage data, model data, or analytics. There are no background processes, no internet connections, and no telemetry of any kind built into the plugin.

The plugin never communicates with any external server — not during installation, not during use, and not during license activation.

3. Local Storage

Auto Dimension Pro stores the following information locally on your machine only:

  • Trial period tracking — a timestamp recorded in your Windows AppData folder and registry, used solely to enforce the 7-day trial period. This information never leaves your machine.
  • Plugin settings — your last-used options (offsets and dimension types), saved in your Windows AppData folder so you don't need to reconfigure the plugin each time you open it. This information never leaves your machine.

4. License Activation

License activation is handled manually and entirely offline. When you purchase a license, you provide your Machine ID to the publisher by email. The publisher sends back an activation key. The key is validated locally on your machine with no server call required.

Your email address is used only to deliver your license key and is not shared with any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

Auto Dimension Pro does not integrate with, or send data to, any third-party service, analytics platform, or cloud provider.

6. Children's Privacy

Auto Dimension Pro is a professional software tool intended for use by adults in an architectural or engineering context. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All data stored by Auto Dimension Pro exists only on your local machine and is retained until you uninstall the plugin or delete the files manually.

Uninstalling the plugin via Windows Settings → Apps → Auto Dimension Pro → Uninstall removes the plugin files. Local data files, including trial period tracking, plugin settings, and license information, are stored in your Windows AppData folder under a dedicated Auto Dimension Pro subfolder. You may delete this folder manually at any time to remove all stored data.

8. Revoking Consent & Requesting Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your machine and never transmitted to any server, you have full control over it at all times.

To delete all data stored by Auto Dimension Pro, uninstall the plugin using Windows Settings → Apps → Auto Dimension Pro → Uninstall, then delete the Auto Dimension Pro subfolder in your Windows AppData folder.

To revoke consent or request confirmation that no personal data is held about you, contact the publisher at larafayounes@gmail.com. Since no personal data is stored on any server, there is nothing to delete remotely, but the publisher will confirm this in writing upon request.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Continued use of the plugin after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
